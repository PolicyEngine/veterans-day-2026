Of the 41 states with a broad income tax, 28 exempt all military retired pay in 2026. The other 13 tax some of it, depending on the retiree's age, income, years of service or work, and the District of Columbia taxes all of it. Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming levy no broad income tax.

For example, take a married couple in which one spouse is 45, draws $30,000 of military retired pay and earns $60,000 in a civilian job. The couple owes more state income tax because of the pay in {{nstates_plus_dc:working_45}} states and DC, from {{mintax:working_45}} in {{minstate:working_45}} to {{maxtax:working_45}} in {{maxstate:working_45}}. A retired couple aged 67 and 65, living on $45,000 of military retired pay and $30,000 of Social Security, owes more in {{nstates_plus_dc:retired_67}} states and DC, up to {{maxtax:retired_67}} in {{maxstate:retired_67}}.

## How we calculated these

We calculated each couple's 2026 state income tax in every state twice with [PolicyEngine-US](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-us), once with the military retired pay and once without it. Both couples file jointly, claim no dependents and live in the state all year. Each retiree served 20 years and has no service-connected disability. The 67-year-old served entirely before 1998, and 177 of those 240 months fall before October 1991. In Montana, we assume the retiree meets the exemption's residency condition and has claimed it for fewer than five years. We count state credits, including refundable ones, in state income tax and leave out local income taxes, such as those Maryland's counties levy. Several states have yet to publish their 2026 inflation adjustments, and PolicyEngine-US estimates those amounts.

PolicyEngine's microdata record VA payments but not veteran status or military retired pay, so we don't estimate how many retirees each state's rules reach. We publish the code and full results, including a wider grid of ages, pay and wages, at [github.com/PolicyEngine/veterans-day-2026](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/veterans-day-2026).
